Qrow, three directions

Each mark is shown as a lockup and at 16, 32, 48 and 96 pixels, on paper and on the ink board. Coral marks the one element that stands for the payment.

A. Sealed

An envelope held shut by a round seal. The payment stays sealed until the recipient opens it; QoreSeal carries the notice.

Rounded monoline lowercase, round caps.

Critique

Works
Closest to the product: a payment that stays sealed until the recipient opens it, and it ties to QoreSeal, the encrypted notice. The coral seal is the one element that reads as 'the money'.
Risks
An envelope is the universal mail icon, so next to a wallet or an inbox it can read as 'messages', not 'payments'. Says nothing about refunds or quantum safety.
At 16px
At 16px it holds as a rectangle with a dot; the flap lines thin out. Legible, but generic.
Scores
  • Concept 4/5
  • originality 2/5
  • 16px 3/5
  • light/dark 5/5

B. Return

One continuous stroke: a Q whose tail runs out and loops back into the ring. It goes out; unclaimed, it comes home.

The symbol is the capital Q, then “row” in the same monoline.

Critique

Works
The only mark that is also the letter, so the symbol and the wordmark are one idea. One continuous stroke tells the refund story: it goes out, and if nobody claims it, it comes back.
Risks
The looped tail can read as a speech bubble or a map pin if drawn too round; the loop must stay slim. Quantum safety is not in the mark, it lives in the product.
At 16px
Best at 16px: a ring with a tail is a Q at any size, and a single stroke has nothing to fill in.
Scores
  • Concept 4/5
  • originality 4/5
  • 16px 5/5
  • light/dark 5/5

C. Lattice

A 3×3 lattice with one cell set out. ML-DSA is lattice cryptography; eight cells hold, one payment moves.

Square-cut monoline lowercase, butt caps, mitred joins.

Critique

Works
The most honest quantum-safe reference (ML-DSA is lattice cryptography) and the square-cut wordmark has real character. The coral cell is a clear 'one payment moves'.
Risks
A 3×3 grid of squares is the 'all apps' launcher icon and a phone keypad; most senders will not know what a lattice is, so the meaning needs explaining. Feels technical rather than calm.
At 16px
At 16px the nine cells blur into a grey square with a coral dot; it survives as a shape, not as a lattice.
Scores
  • Concept 3/5
  • originality 2/5
  • 16px 3/5
  • light/dark 5/5
