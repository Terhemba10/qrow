# Qrow demo — story plan (video-storytelling)

## Shape
A product demo / build-along: one user goes from nothing to a verified, quantum-safe payment. Skill shape:
"tutorial or build-along" → **the finished journey, ghosted; every step not yet done**.

## One persistent world: the journey rail
A slim rail across the top of the frame, present from the hook to the end, never redrawn or moved:

`CONNECT → FAUCET → SWAP → SEND → CLAIM → VERIFY`

- **Planted** in the hook (all six ghosted), on the line that makes the promise.
- **Advanced** at every boundary: the current step lights (spotlight), done steps stay quiet but legible, steps
  ahead stay ghosted. Re-shown at each scene change (~2 s) as the retention mechanic.
- **Closed** on VERIFY with the receipt's hash, then the whole rail lit once at the end card (the payoff pull-back).
- Spatial grammar: left → right is toward "verified on chain". Never reversed.

## The thing to follow
The **1 fUSD payment**: typed on Send, waits in escrow, claimed on Receive, proven on Verify.

## Counter with a name
Corner chip, numeral + name, rolling as one fixture: `01 CONNECT`, `02 FAUCET`, `03 SWAP`, `04 SEND`, `05 CLAIM`,
`06 VERIFY`.

## Spotlight
Exactly one thing at full brightness: the field being used (camera push), with one label pointing at it. Rail and
counter sit quiet while the footage works.

## Discipline
- Every label is 1–5 words and names an action ("Type the amount here").
- About 20 on-screen words per scene, beyond the captions.
- No cross-fades on full-frame layers: cut or clip-wipe.
- Camera move first, then any wipe; one gesture, not two.
- Nothing within 60 px of the frame edge.

## Script proposals (need the owner's OK before any voice line is regenerated)
1. **s01 hook, plant the promise** (adds about 2.5 s; the scene is 12 s, the clip would be about 10.5 s):
   - Now: "One day, quantum computers will break the signatures that protect today's crypto payments. Qrow is built for that day."
   - Proposed: "One day, quantum computers will break the signatures that protect today's crypto payments. Qrow is built for that day. Watch one payment, start to finish."
2. **s07 verify, close the loop**, only if s07 shows the owner's own payment (re-recorded with its QRW-PAY reference
   after the owner's Send clip exists):
   - Now: "Every action ends with a receipt and its transaction hash. Paste any hash into Verify, and Qrow reads it straight from the testnet: …"
   - Proposed: "Every action ends with a receipt and its transaction hash. Paste it into Verify, and Qrow reads it straight from the testnet: …"

Everything else stays: the hook has real stakes, every scene names a concrete action, and the end card answers the
opening line.
