One day,
quantum computers will break the signatures that protect today's crypto payments.
Qrow is built for that day.
Qrow is an escrow payments app on QoreChain testnet.
Every payment waits safely in escrow until it's claimed,
and every step is signed with a quantum-safe key, ML-DSA-87.
You connect QoreX once.
New here?
The Faucet tab gets you free test QOR and fUSD from QoreChain's official faucet.
Swap trades on QoreChain's built-in exchange.
Qrow quotes from the chain itself, checks your balance and the price impact before you sign,
then asks for one approval in QoreX.
Sending is two clicks and one approval.
Qrow checks the address for lookalikes,
then the money waits in escrow for up to seven days.
The first time, that same approval also sets up this device's quantum-safe key.
Nothing to write down, nothing to save.
The recipient claims it with their own quantum-safe signature.
If nobody claims it in time, it goes back to the sender.
Every action ends with a receipt and its transaction hash.
Paste any hash into Verify, and Qrow reads it straight from the testnet: who signed,
the quantum-safe check the chain ran, and what the contract did.
Portfolio, NFTs, staking and a coin launcher with anti-scam checks round it out.
